Ticket lifecycle

How a ticket moves from creation through resolution and closure.

OPEN Customer reports an issue and creates a support ticket.
ASSIGNED Support team assigns the ticket to the right technician or department.
IN_PROGRESS Technician investigates the issue and reviews customer service info.
RESOLVED The issue is fixed remotely or a technician visit is scheduled.
CLOSED Customer confirms the fix and the ticket is completed.
REOPENED If the problem persists, the ticket returns to investigation.
